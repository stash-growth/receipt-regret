Frequently Asked Questions

Is this real investing?

This is a demo that shows hypothetical calculations. The tool illustrates what might have happened if you'd invested instead of spending—but it's not actual investing.

Real investing requires opening an account with a brokerage or investment platform, choosing specific funds or assets, and managing ongoing contributions. This demo is meant to inspire better financial habits, not replace professional guidance.

Why show long-term growth?

Small, everyday purchases add up over time. By visualizing how those dollars could have grown with compound interest, we help you see the long-term impact of your spending choices.

The goal isn't to cause regret—it's to inspire forward-thinking habits. Even modest, consistent investing can build meaningful wealth over years.

What about fees and taxes?

This demo uses a simplified 7% CAGR and does not factor in investment fees, taxes, or inflation. In reality, you'd need to consider:

  • Management fees — Funds and platforms charge fees that reduce net returns
  • Taxes — Capital gains, dividends, and withdrawals may be taxable
  • Inflation — The purchasing power of your money changes over time

For accurate planning, consult with a financial advisor or use tools that account for these factors.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. All calculations in this demo run entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server, and no data is stored or tracked.

You can verify this by opening your browser's Network tab in Developer Tools—you'll see no API calls are made when you submit the form. This is a privacy-first, client-side demo.

Protect Your Privacy

Before uploading a receipt, consider these tips to protect your personal information:

  • Blur sensitive details — Use your phone's photo editor to blur credit card numbers, full names, or addresses before uploading.
  • Crop what you don't need — Focus on just the transaction details (amount, merchant, date).
  • Check the image — Make sure no sensitive info is visible in the background or reflections.

Remember: This is a demo. Your images stay on your device and are never uploaded to a server.
